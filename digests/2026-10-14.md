# Engineering Digest — 2026-10-14

Two fresh CI/mobile postmortems, a platform-policy rethink, wasted GPU fleets, and a RAG reality check on ingestion and recall. The theme of the day: **incident reviews and RAG foundations**.

## Incident reviews

### 1. Post Incident Report: Oct 6, 2026 — Delays in pipelines, workflows, UI data, and notifications — CircleCI
https://discuss.circleci.com/t/post-incident-report-oct-6-2026-delays-in-pipelines-workflows-ui-data-and-notifications/54908

Routine database maintenance (anti-wraparound autovacuum) wrote transaction logs faster than they could be archived, filling the WAL volume and stalling every pipeline; the fix meant taking the DB offline for 50 minutes. Worth reading for anyone whose orchestration depends on a single stateful store — it shows how background hygiene jobs become outage vectors.

### 2. The Pulse: Firebase's global outage and poor response — Pragmatic Engineer
https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/the-pulse-firebases-global-outage-poor-response/

A stale config flag cleanup shipped a malformed payload that crashed iOS apps on launch worldwide, while Firebase's status dashboards stayed green because they only track server-side health. A cautionary tale about client-side blast radius: your backend can be healthy and your users still down.

## Platform engineering

### 3. Guardrails, not gates: rethinking policy in platform teams — Koray Oksay / CNCF
https://www.cncf.io/blog/2026/10/01/guardrails-not-gates-rethinking-policy-in-platform-teams/

Argues that Kubernetes policy tooling built around 'deny' turns platform teams into appeals courts and pushes developers toward shadow infrastructure. For anyone running a platform: design policy as guidance with defaults, not as a wall, or adoption quietly dies.

## Cloud infrastructure

### 4. Your Kubernetes GPUs Are Sitting 98% Idle. Here's the Data — Vortex 404 / DevOps.dev
https://blog.devops.dev/your-kubernetes-gpus-are-sitting-98-idle-heres-the-data-4a589a809d05?gi=aa2f38323e60

Cast.ai's 2026 report measured 23,000 real clusters: average GPU utilization is 5% (AKS 2%, EKS 5%, GKE 6%), so teams pay for ~20x the GPU they use. Essential ammunition if your org provisions GPU capacity by vibes instead of measured demand.

## Spring Boot

### 5. Spring Boot Performance Tuning: What to Fix Before You Waste Money on More Servers — Engineering Playbook / Medium
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/spring-boot-performance-tuning-what-to-fix-before-you-waste-money-on-more-servers-f91c0b7c41da

A measurement-first checklist for slow Spring Boot APIs: identify the constrained resource before adding replicas, and treat database queries, transaction length, and retry policy as the usual suspects. A good antidote to the 'just scale it' reflex on under-tuned services.

## RAG engineering

### 6. Structural Content Extraction: The Missing Layer in Production RAG — Sergej Schweizer
https://medium.com/@sergej.schweizer/structural-content-extraction-the-missing-layer-in-production-rag-442c678d186b

Argues that flattening documents to plain text before chunking destroys the headings, tables, and metadata that downstream retrieval needs most. If your RAG pipeline ingests PDFs or HTML, read this before tuning embeddings — the structure is lost at ingestion, not at search.

### 7. My Experiments with RAG Retrieval: Here's What Actually Improved Recall — Jafar Ali Habshee
https://medium.com/@j.ali.hab/my-experiments-with-rag-retrieval-heres-what-actually-improved-recall-ec9ee4fdb9ef

Hands-on experiments on a proprietary dataset showing which retrieval tweaks actually moved recall — and that chunk size alone rarely does. A practical counterpoint to theory-heavy RAG posts, useful for anyone tuning a retrieval pipeline against real measurements.
