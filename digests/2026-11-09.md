# Engineering Digest — 2026-11-09

Six reads worth your time today. The theme of the day: **idempotency, pool limits, idle fleets**.

## Distributed Systems

### 1. Your Payment System Says Success. Your Finance Team Says $400,000 Is Missing — Software Engineering Knowledge (Medium)
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/your-payment-system-says-success-your-finance-team-says-400-000-is-missing-7ef41b6ddf69

A timeout does not mean a payment failed, so a retry with a fresh ID can charge the customer twice while every dashboard stays green. The fix is stable idempotency keys plus reconciliation across ledger, provider, and accounting. For anyone building services that move money.

### 2. 47 Microservices, 9 Engineers, and a CTO Who Called It Scalability — Staff Engineer (Medium)
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/47-microservices-9-engineers-and-a-cto-who-called-it-scalability-61b0b6830748

Eleven synchronous calls per checkout meant a notification-service blip rejected paid orders. The lesson: separate business operations from each other and make non-critical calls asynchronous. For teams weighing whether their service count matches their headcount.

## Java / Spring Boot

### 3. Spring Boot HikariCP Connection Pool Exhausted: 9 Causes That Actually Matter in Production — Software Engineering Knowledge (Medium)
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/spring-boot-hikaricp-connection-pool-exhausted-9-causes-that-actually-matter-in-production-6d20b6c954a9

Pool exhaustion is a symptom, not the disease: slow queries, long-held connections, and stalled downstreams are the usual culprits, and doubling maxPoolSize often makes the incident worse. For Spring Boot owners who have seen that 30-second timeout.

### 4. 5 JPA/Hibernate Mistakes That Quietly Kill Your Spring Boot Performance — Vitalii Sukach (Medium)
https://medium.com/@sukach.v.v/5-jpa-hibernate-mistakes-that-quietly-kill-your-spring-boot-performance-99004483ac51

Open Session in View holding a connection for 320 ms per request caps a service at roughly 31 requests per second on a 10-connection pool, while every query still looks fast. Five such defaults, each quantified. For anyone whose API is slow while the database looks bored.

## Cloud Infrastructure

### 5. EKS Powers the Cloud's Largest Kubernetes Fleets — So Why Is It Still Running Mostly Idle? — Cloud Native Now
https://cloudnativenow.com/features/eks-powers-the-clouds-largest-kubernetes-fleets-so-why-is-it-still-running-mostly-idle/

Across tens of thousands of clusters, average CPU utilization fell to 8% while overprovisioning hit 69%, and GPU utilization sits at 5% — defensive resource requests plus autoscaler limits explain the waste. For platform teams paying for capacity their apps never use.

## Applied AI / RAG

### 6. Your RAG System Isn't Failing Because of the LLM. It's Failing Before the Prompt. — Krish (Medium)
https://medium.com/@thekrish/your-rag-system-isnt-failing-because-of-the-llm-it-s-failing-before-the-prompt-e9afe0843f9c

Retrieval quality, document preparation, and permissions decide RAG outcomes before the model ever sees a token. The piece lays out a three-part evaluation — retrieval, answer, operations — instead of chasing a better model. For teams whose RAG demo works and whose production answers don't.
