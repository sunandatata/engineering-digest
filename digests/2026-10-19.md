# Engineering Digest — 2026-10-19

Real numbers week: a virtual-thread incident at 2 AM, two measured RAG bake-offs, and the hard truth about Kubernetes utilization. The theme of the day: **virtual threads vs the pool, RAG measured, K8s cost war**.

## Java / Spring Boot

### 1. Java Virtual Threads in Production: What Nobody Tells You — Naveen Metta
https://naveen-metta.medium.com/java-virtual-threads-in-production-what-nobody-tells-you-f237299d7ff2

A Spring Boot order service died at 2:14 AM with 40,000 virtual threads alive and CPU at 9 percent: flipping one property removed the thread-pool backpressure and every virtual thread piled onto a 30-connection pool. The fix is guarding scarce resources with explicit semaphores, not bigger pools. For anyone enabling virtual threads in Spring Boot 3.x.

## Applied AI / RAG

### 2. Finding every needle in a haystack: improving RAG recall from 38% to 92% — Marc Fasel
https://medium.com/@marc_fasel/finding-every-needle-in-a-haystack-improving-rag-recall-from-38-to-92-475e30cb303d

Traced every lost needle through a RAG pipeline against a known synthetic ground truth: the production-like setup managed 37 percent answer recall, and the obvious levers (bigger top-k, more context, query expansion) moved it by 5 needles at most. A map-reduce shape, one LLM call per deal plus a final synthesis call, reached 92 percent recall at 100 percent precision. Great example of measuring retrieval before tuning the model.

### 3. I Tested 9 RAG Architectures on the Same Dataset. Here Are the Retrieval Accuracy Scores. — Sakshi
https://iffywhy.medium.com/i-tested-9-rag-architectures-on-the-same-dataset-here-are-the-retrieval-accuracy-scores-b86d97366e07

Naive RAG, hybrid plus rerank, HyDE, CRAG, Self-RAG, GraphRAG, Adaptive RAG, RAPTOR and Agentic RAG, all scored on the same 200-question set with one embedding model and one evaluator, so the retrieval accuracy numbers are actually comparable. If you have ever wondered which architecture upgrade is worth the complexity for your data, this is the closest thing to an answer.

### 4. The RAG Evaluation Reckoning Has Arrived - and Your Retrieval Stack — Capernaum
https://www.capernaums.com/d/digest-retrieval-search-1791101800.html

Surveys the arrival of serious RAG evaluation infrastructure: RAGBench makes failures attributable across retrieval, ranker and generator, BERGEN standardizes the pipeline grid, and a systematic review finds two years of scattered, incompatible metrics. The uncomfortable takeaway: shipping RAG without a decomposable eval harness is now visibly behind the state of the art. For leads budgeting next quarter's eval work.

## Cloud Infrastructure

### 5. Kubernetes Cost Optimization: Real-World Strategies That Actually Work — Garrett Yan
https://dev.to/garrett_yan/kubernetes-cost-optimization-real-world-strategies-that-actually-work-6f3

Six strategies applied in sequence over six weeks on an EKS cluster: pod right-sizing, Karpenter, spot instances, Graviton, descheduler, and cost visibility dashboards. Result was 14.2k to 4.18k dollars a month, a 71 percent cut, with no downtime and no application rewrites. The hardest part was organizational: making cost a visible team-level metric.

### 6. Kubernetes cost wars, two years in — Rajan Sharma
https://therajansharma.medium.com/kubernetes-cost-wars-two-years-in-e9f740e82417

Two years into the Kubernetes cost-optimization era, average cluster CPU utilization fell to a record low near 10 percent: the industry spent heavily on tooling and FinOps and got less efficient. Argues the waste is structural, not laziness, and separates what is genuinely working from what is dashboard theater. A sobering companion to any rightsizing plan.
