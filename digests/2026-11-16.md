# Engineering Digest — 2026-11-16

Today's picks share one lesson: the number on the dashboard is rarely the whole story — whether it's a smooth latency graph, a bigger connection pool, or a demo that works. The theme of the day: **Hidden costs, quiet failures, measured fixes**.

## Distributed Systems

### 1. Don't Smooth the System — Kadir Atesoglu
https://atesoglu.medium.com/dont-smooth-the-system-dff81bef8286

Averages and clean dashboards hide the variability that actually explains failures. Argues for systems that stay legible when they misbehave, not just when they're healthy — for anyone who reviews architectures or owns observability.

### 2. Microservices Communication Patterns: RPC, Messaging, and Managing Dependencies — Jin Vincent Necesario
https://lock29down.medium.com/microservices-communication-patterns-rpc-messaging-and-managing-dependencies-e91746cda064

Walks through RPC vs. message-driven communication, service discovery, and versioning, with latency and availability as the deciding forces. A solid primer for anyone designing how services talk to each other.

## Java/Spring Boot

### 3. We Increased HikariCP From 20 to 100 Connections. PostgreSQL Got Five Times Slower — Devrim Ozcay
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/we-increased-hikaricp-from-20-to-100-connections-postgresql-got-five-times-slower-d43733bcd88b

Raising the pool from 20 to 100 connections made PostgreSQL five times slower: a pool is a queue, not capacity, and more concurrency against a saturated database burns throughput. Essential reading for Spring Boot + Postgres teams.

### 4. Spring Boot Production Performance: Fix p99 Latency, PostgreSQL Locks, and Kafka Failures — Systems at Scale
https://medium.com/javarevisited/spring-boot-production-performance-fix-p99-latency-postgresql-locks-and-kafka-failures-087d9b3ee209

Traces one incident — 80ms to 12s p99 with every dashboard green — through lock waits, a slow payment provider, and Kafka duplicates to show how the symptoms share a single failure chain. For senior engineers debugging production latency.

## Cloud Infrastructure

### 5. OpenCost and Kubernetes FinOps: Real Costs, Detecting Cryptojacking, and the Resource-Security Loop — Yuval Glasman, Platform Monkey
https://platform-monkey.com/guides/opencost-finops-k8s

Shows how OpenCost attributes real cost per workload with a max(requested, used) model, and how FinOps metrics can double as an early warning for cryptojacking. For platform teams who need defensible per-team bills.

## Applied AI/RAG

### 6. A Production RAG Pipeline, End to End — Yamraj Khadka
https://www.yamrajkhadka.com.np/writing/rag-pipeline

Treats RAG as a data pipeline with a model attached — ingestion, chunking, hybrid retrieval, reranking, citations, evaluation — and walks the whole shape. For engineers building RAG over private or specialised corpora.

### 7. Why Your RAG Pipeline Looks Great in Demos and Falls Apart in Production — Sudoofe
https://medium.com/@sudoofe123/why-your-rag-pipeline-looks-great-in-demos-and-falls-apart-in-production-de5e56af84e6

Demos hide the failure modes that show up at scale: stale retrieval, bad chunks, hallucination even with the right documents in context. Frames evaluation as an ongoing metric, not a one-time check — for teams moving a RAG demo to real users.
