# Engineering Digest — 2026-11-02

Seven reads worth your time today. The theme of the day: **measure the bottleneck first**.

## Distributed Systems

### 1. What decades of networking and distributed systems engineering can teach us about AI inference — Sean Fraser
https://medium.com/@cloudhacks_/ai-inference-systems-engineering-b995bdf1c77d

Inference serving is a scheduling, memory, and queueing problem in new clothes: find the real bottleneck before optimizing, and test each change against a fixed workload. Best for engineers moving distributed-systems instincts into LLM serving.

### 2. Netflix, Uber and Spotify Event Pipelines: Real Numbers From Their Own Engineering Posts, and What to Copy in Your Kafka Consumers — Navya Devadiga
https://medium.com/system-design-architecture-deep-dives/netflix-uber-and-spotify-event-pipelines-real-numbers-and-what-to-copy-in-your-kafka-consumers-6518ca6a4ca5

A date-stamped audit of famous pipeline numbers, correcting misattributed figures, then four practical ideas for Kafka consumers. Worth reading before quoting a scale number in a design review.

## Java / Spring Boot

### 3. Spring Boot Production Troubleshooting: 15 Real Failure Scenarios and Fixes (2026) — Systems at Scale
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/spring-boot-production-troubleshooting-15-real-failure-scenarios-and-fixes-2026-be3efb955b71

Fifteen failure patterns where the visible symptom is rarely the cause, from HikariCP exhaustion to Kafka lag traced back to a database. A useful on-call reference for tracing a symptom to its mechanism instead of restarting everything.

### 4. Spring Boot 3 EOL to Spring Boot 4: A Production Upgrade Playbook — Mirfan Arshad
https://mirfanarshad.medium.com/spring-boot-3-eol-to-spring-boot-4-a-production-upgrade-playbook-35d5b542c88a

Treat Boot 4 as two migrations in one version number: platform baselines, starter changes, and Jackson 2 to 3, with a phased rollout checklist. For teams still on Boot 3 weighing EOL and CVE pressure.

## Cloud Infrastructure

### 5. Two Traefik Bugs, One Broken Boundary: Kubernetes Namespace Isolation Research — Lav Kumar Vishwakarma
https://medium.com/@lav.kumar.dev/two-traefik-bugs-one-broken-boundary-kubernetes-namespace-isolation-research-d263739bc2f9

Two namespace-isolation bugs in Traefik's Kubernetes providers show how an internal identifier can let one tenant clobber another's routing or TLS options. A sharp reminder to check identifier uniqueness across tenants in any multi-tenant control plane.

### 6. Builds, deploys, and queued deploys are not starting in all regions — Render
https://postmortem.io/incidents/render--2026-10-14--job-scheduler-schema-migration-builds-deploys/

A planned job-scheduler upgrade's schema migration stalled builds and deploys across regions for over two hours, with recovery slowed by a still-running migration and a failed capacity add. Read it before running a migration on a system that coordinates everything else.

## Applied AI / RAG

### 7. RAG in production in 2026: patterns and evaluation — BixTech
https://bixtech.ai/rag-in-production-2026/

Production RAG fails at retrieval, not at the model: hybrid search plus reranking, evaluated separately for retrieval and generation, with cost and latency as release gates. A compact decision frame for teams deciding between more context, better retrieval, or fine-tuning.
