# Engineering Digest — 2026-11-14

Today's picks: why doubling your database connections can slow you down, what a 68% AWS bill jump really buys, and why retrieval — not prompting — caps your RAG system. The theme of the day: **Pool sizing lies, Kubernetes bills, and the RAG ceiling**.

## Distributed Systems

### 1. 5 Production Mistakes Even 10+ Year Backend Engineers Still Make — Sopheaktra Yorn
https://medium.com/@yornsopheaktra/5-production-mistakes-even-10-year-backend-engineers-still-make-e52fe3039c83

A checklist of the traps that survive a decade of experience: network calls inside open transactions, retries without jitter, and OFFSET pagination past a million rows. Best used as a pre-merge review prompt for any backend team.

### 2. Engineering Correctness in Distributed Systems — Alex Chang
https://medium.com/@herefindalex/engineering-correctness-in-distributed-systems-33e2d9950dfb

A methodical walkthrough of defining invariants, drawing consistency boundaries, and modeling failures before they become production behavior — with TLA+ and IronFleet as worked references. For engineers who want rigor, not just rules of thumb.

## Java/Spring Boot

### 3. We Doubled Our Database Connections. The API Got Slower. Here's What We Missed — Devrim Ozcay
https://medium.com/javarevisited/we-doubled-our-database-connections-the-api-got-slower-heres-what-we-missed-0936de3e661b

A Spring Boot service at 600 req/s got slower after HikariCP's pool grew: the bottleneck had moved into PostgreSQL, where it was harder to see. The lesson is to find why the pool saturated before touching its size — a must-read before your next pool-size tweak.

### 4. The Senior Engineer Who Made Our API 10 Times Faster by Deleting Code — Production War Stories
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/the-senior-engineer-who-made-our-api-10-times-faster-by-deleting-code-726a0c5504e3

Six months of JVM tuning and pod scaling lost to one engineer deleting 600 lines and fixing an N+1 query — checkout p99 fell from 4.2s to ~410ms. A reminder that the most expensive performance problems often look like clean code.

## Cloud Infrastructure

### 5. We Moved Our Spring Boot API to Kubernetes. Our AWS Bill Jumped 68% — Beyond Senior
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/we-moved-our-spring-boot-api-to-kubernetes-our-aws-bill-jumped-68-eabcd1c2f542

The migration passed every performance test, then finance asked about the bill: cross-AZ traffic and NAT gateways, not compute, drove the increase. Essential reading before pricing any EKS move — the networking design is the bill.

## Applied AI/RAG

### 6. RAG in Production: Retrieval Is Data Engineering, Not a Prompt Trick — André Claudino
https://medium.com/@andrelgcclaudino/rag-in-production-retrieval-is-data-engineering-not-a-prompt-trick-d07c3defe223

Your grounded answer can never beat the retrieval that fed it — so measure Recall@K and ranking before touching prompts, and build a 50–200 example golden set first. The clearest framing yet of retrieval as a data-engineering problem.

### 7. Production Problems in RAG and How to Solve Them — Live Demo — Vishal Mysore
https://medium.com/@visrow/production-problems-in-rag-and-how-to-solve-them-live-demo-9e785d025ccb

Nine silent failures that appear after day one — stale vectors, mixed versions after a crash, answers served from yesterday's cache — each replayable in a browser lab. Turning on the first four fixes cut a simulated week's re-embedding cost from 25,800 to 100.
