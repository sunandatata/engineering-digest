# Engineering Digest — 2026-10-15

Six reads worth your time today. The theme of the day: **queues, chunking, and real costs**.

## Distributed Systems

### 1. Kafka Just Learned to Be a Queue. Postgres Has Been One All Along. — dev.to
https://dev.to/firfircelik/kafka-just-learned-to-be-a-queue-postgres-has-been-one-all-along-2i9g

A head-to-head of Kafka's new share groups (KIP-932) against the old FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED queue pattern, compared on claim semantics, crash behavior, poison messages, and DLQs — with the honest caveat that Python/Go clients are still Preview. For anyone choosing the work-queue substrate under their next service.

### 2. Why Your Kafka Consumer Keeps Getting Kicked Out — The Lazy Senior (YouTube)
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xn7L5CFweu8

A deliberately broken Kafka 4.3.1 cluster, measured end to end: consumers kicked every 6 minutes with 0 commits, then the usual session.timeout bump making things worse, then the real fix — shrinking max.poll.records from 500 to 100 — taking lag from 6,000 to 600 with zero duplicates. Essential if you have ever stared at a 'member revoked' rebalance.

## Java & Spring

### 3. Build an AI Agent with Spring AI 2.0: A Working Example with Guardrails — patotski.com
https://patotski.com/blog/build-ai-agent-spring-ai/

A complete, runnable Spring AI 2.0.1 agent with the two checks that matter: a tool-call cap check so the loop's limit error never reaches your customer, and a proposal check so refund-like actions hit a human before they execute. For Java engineers turning Spring services into agentic ones.

## RAG & Applied AI

### 4. Agentic RAG Production Guide: Architecture & Real Costs — jahanzaib.ai
https://www.jahanzaib.ai/blog/agentic-rag-production-guide

Recomputed-for-October-2026 pricing: roughly $1,860-$2,850/month in LLM API costs at 1,000 queries/day, plus vector store minimums, with per-query cost split between simple and multi-hop retrievals. Pairs the numbers with a blunt decision rule — many teams that think they need agentic RAG actually need better chunking first.

### 5. Advanced RAG Chunking: Hierarchical, Semantic, and Contextual Retrieval — Anchal Nigam (Towards AI)
https://pub.towardsai.net/advanced-rag-chunking-hierarchical-semantic-and-contextual-retrieval-2e61dd08a225

Part 5 of a production RAG series tackling the split that defines real systems: tiny snippets for retrieval precision against full documents for safe answers. Covers semantic chunking, parent-document retrieval, breadcrumbs, and Anthropic's contextual retrieval — directly applicable to anyone whose corpus is messy transcripts and policy docs.

## Cloud Infrastructure

### 6. From 4 Months to 2 Years: Scaling Systems and Stepping into Seniority at Bazaarvoice — Bazaarvoice Engineering
https://blog.developer.bazaarvoice.com/2026/09/29/from-4-months-to-2-years-scaling-systems-and-stepping-into-seniority-at-bazaarvoice/

A field report on modernizing at real scale: 100+ Kubernetes deployments moved from self-managed KOPS to EKS AutoMode, AWS cost posture tightened through tagging and redesign, Jenkins pipelines migrated to GitHub Actions, plus an internal developer platform to carry it. For platform engineers planning the same journey.
