# Engineering Digest — 2026-10-22

Six pieces on failure containment: how small failures travel, when your platform locks you out of the fix, GPU waste you can measure, and the retrieval discipline that separates demos from systems. The theme of the day: **failure radius, rotation traps, retrieval discipline**.

## Distributed Systems

### 1. Ten Ways Small Failures Become Production Outages: Failure Radius in Distributed Systems — JIN
https://jinlow.medium.com/ten-ways-small-failures-become-production-outages-failure-radius-in-distributed-systems-0ab70e3a2a05

A postmortem study of how retries, health checks, autoscalers, and redelivery — each doing its job correctly — combine to kill a system. Reliability work is deciding in advance how far each failure is allowed to travel.

## Cloud Infrastructure

### 2. When Managed Platforms Fall: Postmortem of the Railway Security Incident and the PaaS Rotation Trap — Arijit Roy
https://medium.com/@arijitroy0445/when-managed-platforms-fall-postmortem-of-the-railway-security-incident-and-the-paas-rotation-trap-74844769ab51

After a breach exposed Railway's secret store, engineers couldn't rotate credentials because builds were paused globally — the 'rotation trap.' A real, current incident that argues for secret architectures that never lock you out of remediation.

## Java/Spring Boot

### 3. 10 Spring Boot Mistakes That Pass Code Review but Fail in Production — Makara SENG
https://medium.com/@makara.seng/10-spring-boot-mistakes-that-pass-code-review-but-fail-in-production-0e8157063991

Ten familiar patterns — silent @Transactional, N+1 queries, unbounded endpoints — each checked against the Spring and Hibernate reference docs, with small models to show the damage in numbers. Rare for this genre: it flags its own corrected claims.

## Cloud Infrastructure

### 4. How We Cut AI Infrastructure Costs by 40% Through Kubernetes GPU Optimization — Ujjwal Trivedi
https://medium.com/@ujjwalt429/how-we-cut-ai-infrastructure-costs-by-40-through-kubernetes-gpu-optimization-7f27c522b142

Average GPU utilization at 25-30% with full GPUs handed to pods serving a few requests a minute — the DCGM-in-Prometheus measurement, GPU slicing, and scale-to-zero discipline that cut 40% off the bill without slowing data scientists.

## AI/RAG Engineering

### 5. What Production RAG Needs That the Demo Doesn't — _Prav (TechDelta)
https://techdelta.medium.com/what-production-rag-needs-that-the-demo-doesnt-32373e32d4df

Lessons from running retrieval inside live phone calls: untrusted documents, one index shared across tenants, a caller waiting on every answer — and the hard truth that when retrieval fails, nothing raises an error; the model just sounds confident.

### 6. Embeddings and Retrieval in RAG, What Breaks in Production and How to Fix It — Akshat Maurya
https://medium.com/@akshatmaurya25/embeddings-and-retrieval-in-rag-what-breaks-in-production-and-how-to-fix-it-ec9fd5580e71

Production retrieval quality comes from chunking, hybrid retrieval, reranking, and honest evals — not the embedding model. The discipline to adopt: build the golden set first, change one thing at a time, and watch recall@k.
