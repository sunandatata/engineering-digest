# Engineering Digest — 2026-11-01

Six reads on the decisions that turn small failures into big ones. The theme of the day: **rollback traps, hidden platform costs, and retrieval ceilings**.

## Distributed Systems

### 1. I Asked a Staff Engineer to Fix a Production Outage. He Made the Right Call Too Late — ProdRescue
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/i-asked-a-staff-engineer-to-fix-a-production-outage-he-made-the-right-call-too-late-ccc927ef61cd

A checkout incident where rolling back looks obvious until a new Kafka event schema makes the old consumer unsafe. Worth reading for the decision frame: contain harm and protect business invariants first, even when the fix feels clear.

### 2. Production outage lessons: we blamed our code. It was SSL. — Rahul Dhileep Kumar
https://medium.com/@rahuldk1105/production-outage-lessons-we-blamed-our-code-it-was-ssl-e3a92146610d

A backend split that went down for hours turned out to be an expired certificate, not the new code. A useful reminder for small teams to monitor expiry, change one thing at a time, and keep a tested rollback ready.

## Java / Spring Boot

### 3. Spring Boot Performance Tuning: What to Fix Before You Waste Money on More Servers — Software Engineering Knowledge
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/spring-boot-performance-tuning-what-to-fix-before-you-waste-money-on-more-servers-f91c0b7c41da

Before adding replicas, find which resource is actually constrained and why. A practical investigation order for slow Java APIs, aimed at engineers tired of paying for hardware to hide a bottleneck.

## Cloud Infrastructure

### 4. Kubernetes Real World Problems: 12 Production Scenarios — Naveed Ahmed
https://blog.naveedkumbhar.com/kubernetes-scenarios-real-world-problems-solutions/

Twelve cluster postmortems covering the unglamorous killers: CoreDNS latency, IP exhaustion, OOM cascades, and disk pressure. Best used as a diagnostic checklist before assuming an app bug.

### 5. Kubernetes 1.34 Hits End of Life: How to Upgrade EKS, GKE and AKS Without Breaking Production — Qovery
https://www.qovery.com/blog/upgrade-eks-gke-aks-before-kubernetes-1-34-eol

The upgrade risk is rarely the control plane; it is removed APIs, blocked drains, and add-on version skew. The piece gives a safe order across EKS, GKE, and AKS, with node-level rollback as the realistic escape hatch.

## Applied AI / RAG

### 6. Enterprise RAG: Lessons from the Field — Matt Willsmore, Pureinsights
https://pureinsights.com/blog/2026/enterprise-rag-lessons-from-the-field/

Across retail, publishing, and manufacturing pilots, the same layers cap quality: indexing, retrieval, generation, and scale. The highest-return moves are structure-aware chunking, intent routing, and returning verbatim source passages with answers.
