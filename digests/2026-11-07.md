# Engineering Digest — 2026-11-07

When the fix makes the outage worse: scaling traps, delivery guarantees that stop at the boundary, and measuring RAG instead of guessing. The theme of the day: **metastable failures, outbox limits, RAG evals**.

## Distributed Systems

### 1. Our API Latency Hit 18 Seconds. Adding More Kubernetes Pods Made the Outage Worse — The Systems & Architecture (Medium)
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/our-api-latency-hit-18-seconds-adding-more-kubernetes-pods-made-the-outage-worse-0f16f0bd5c95

A SEV-1 where every dashboard looked healthy while checkout latency hit 18 seconds, and autoscaling fed the outage instead of fixing it. A sharp lesson in why low CPU never means spare capacity, and why retries can become your biggest traffic source.

### 2. Our Staff Engineer Added the Outbox Pattern. Kafka Still Processed Orders Twice — Engineering Playbook (Medium)
https://medium.com/engineering-playbook/our-staff-engineer-added-the-outbox-pattern-kafka-still-processed-orders-twice-5671aaff26fe

The transactional outbox worked exactly as designed and orders still shipped twice. A clear walkthrough of where the outbox guarantee ends and why idempotent consumers, not just reliable publishing, decide correctness. Essential for anyone running Spring Boot with Kafka and Debezium.

### 3. The Outage That Outlives Its Trigger — Abhishek Datta (Medium)
https://medium.com/chaos-engineering-field-guide-experiments/the-outage-that-outlives-its-trigger-704844a91a7a

You roll back the bad change and the service stays degraded. Drawing on the HotOS metastable-failure research, this piece explains the mechanisms that keep a system trapped after the trigger is gone, and how to test recovery before you need it.

## Java / Spring Boot

### 4. The Twelve Disciplines of Production Spring — Nick Knapp
https://nick9knapp.org/blog-articles/spring/spring-best-practices.html

With Spring Boot 3.x out of free maintenance, this is a timely tour of the defaults that stop being adequate in production: constructor injection, circular dependencies as design signals, testing against the real database, and how virtual threads reshape the old reactive trade-offs.

## Applied AI / RAG

### 5. RAG Evaluation in 2026: Stop Guessing, Start Measuring — CoderBlog
https://www.coderblog.in/posts/rag-evaluation-2026-stop-guessing-start-measuring.html

A field guide to measuring the three ways RAG breaks: wrong retrieval, hallucination, and ignored context. Covers context recall and precision, label sources from synthetic to mined-from-logs, and why embedding-model swaps need metric stability checks, not vibes.

### 6. Building a RAG Pipeline: A 2026 Engineering Guide — Bill Beltz, QuantLab USA
https://www.quantlabusa.dev/blog/building-a-rag-pipeline-2026

A practitioner walkthrough of the full pipeline, from semantic chunking through retrieve-then-rerank to evaluation. The highest-leverage takeaway: measure retrieval and generation separately, and build a real evaluation set before tuning anything.
