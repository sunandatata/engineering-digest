## About this repo

A daily reading list for software engineers. Entries are picked from recently published engineering writing and selected for substance: production lessons, real numbers, ideas you can actually use.

Every entry links to the original source. Summaries give the curator's take on why a piece is worth your time — all credit for the content belongs to the original authors.

## How curation works

1. Survey recent engineering publications — company engineering blogs, practitioner writing, papers.
2. Filter for production experience over theory: real incidents, real measurements, real trade-offs.
3. Write an original 1–2 line summary per entry: why it matters, and who it's for.

Quality bar: if an entry wouldn't change how you'd build something, it doesn't make the cut.
